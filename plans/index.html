Included, not upgraded

The Leavitt Standard.

Much of what other builders sell as an upgrade is simply how we build. Every home on this page includes all of the following as standard.

Premium interior

  • ✓ 9’ ceilings — basement and first floor
  • ✓ Custom hardwood or luxury vinyl plank flooring — entire first floor
  • ✓ Level 5 drywall finish — in the main living areas, for smooth, flawless walls
  • ✓ Gourmet kitchen — quartz countertops, tile backsplash, premium GE “Café” series appliance allowance
  • ✓ Owner’s retreat — quartz countertops, custom tiled shower walls, tiled to the ceiling
  • ✓ Solid core interior doors — with fully cased windows and openings throughout
  • ✓ Stain grade stairs and handrail — first to second floor
  • ✓ Insulated bedroom, bathroom and laundry walls — for sound dampening

Exterior & infrastructure

  • ✓ High-performance Andersen® windows
  • ✓ Zip System® wall sheathing — for moisture protection
  • ✓ Upgraded siding & 40-year architectural shingles
  • ✓ Stone to grade — front and side elevations
  • ✓ Finished & painted garage — insulated walls and service door
  • ✓ Customer-owned propane tank — topped off at move-in
  • ✓ 75’ × 10’ asphalt drive — includes 30’ × 30’ parking pad and 10’ × 10’ turnaround
  • ✓ Structural integrity — TGI floor joists and Advantech® subflooring, glued and screwed
