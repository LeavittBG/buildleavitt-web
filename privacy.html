Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This policy explains what information Leavitt Building Group (“Leavitt Building Group,” “we,” “us”) collects through buildleavitt.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website only.

Information you give us

The only place this site asks for information is the contact form. When you submit it, we receive:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you choose to provide it
  • The type of project you selected
  • Anything you write in the project details field

Please don’t send sensitive information — such as financial account numbers or government identification numbers — through the contact form. It isn’t the right channel for it.

Information collected automatically

This site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or social media pixels. We do not build profiles of visitors and we do not track you across other websites.

Our web host keeps standard server logs, which typically include IP address, browser type, the pages requested, and the time of the request. These logs are used to operate and secure the site.

Third parties involved in running this site

Loading a page on this site causes your browser to contact:

  • Netlify — hosts the website and processes contact form submissions on our behalf.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — serves the typefaces used on this site. Google receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • unpkg (unpkg.com) — serves the icon set used on this site, and likewise receives your IP address.

These providers handle that data under their own privacy policies. Our pages also link out to Facebook, Instagram, Google Maps, and a client portal; once you follow one of those links, the destination site’s own policies apply.

How we use your information

We use what you send us to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and answer questions
  • Prepare estimates and discuss scope, budget, and timeline
  • Carry out and manage work you engage us for
  • Keep ordinary business records, and meet legal or licensing obligations

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run the business, such as our web host and email provider
  • With architects, designers, suppliers, and trade partners, where doing so is necessary to respond to your inquiry or carry out your project
  • Where we are required to by law, or to establish or defend legal claims

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for as long as needed to respond and to maintain records of our projects and business dealings. Where a project goes ahead, records are kept for the period required by our contracts, insurance, and applicable licensing and tax rules.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email discover@buildleavitt.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. We may need to keep certain records where the law or an existing contract requires it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy law.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No website or method of transmission is completely secure, though, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Please check back from time to time.
