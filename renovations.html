Large-scale renovations and additions.

Breathe new life into the home you already love. We take on whole-house renovations, structural remodels, second stories and new wings, with the same craftsmanship and project management we bring to a new build, so the new spaces sit naturally with the character of the house you have.

Our renovation work is large-scale: the whole house, or a significant part of it, rather than a single room.

What we take on.

  • Whole-house renovations

    Every room, every system and every finish, reworked as one project with one schedule.

  • Structural remodels

    Opening up a floor plan, moving walls and stairs, and the engineering and permits that go with it.

  • Additions

    Second stories, new wings and multi-room additions, built to match the house they join.

How a renovation runs.

  1. 01

    Walk the house

    We start at your home: what works, what does not, and what you want it to be. We talk through budget and timeline before any drawing starts.

  2. 02

    Design & permits

    Drawings for the new layout, your selections from structure to finishes, and the engineering and county permits handled for you.

  3. 03

    Construction

    A dedicated project manager looks after the work and is your point of contact, and Kyle inspects it as it happens. The job site is kept clean, and you get regular progress updates so you always know where things stand.

  4. 04

    The handover

    We walk the finished work with you, test every system and inspect every finish, and fix what we find before we call it done.
