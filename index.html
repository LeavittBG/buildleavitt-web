Words of trust.

Questions we hear most.

How long does it take to build a home?

A semi-custom home takes about 6 to 8 months to complete, depending on the changes made to the plan. A fully custom home takes 10 to 12 months or more, depending on its size and scope.

Do I need to own land first?

No. We build on your lot, or help you find one through our realtor partners.

Do you help with financing?

We do not finance homes ourselves, but we have a banking partner we recommend to help buyers get the best available financing. Who you borrow from is always your choice.

What warranty comes with the home?

Every home comes with a one-year warranty from Leavitt Building Group and a 2-10 Home Buyer's Warranty.

Who will I deal with during the build?

Each home has a dedicated project manager who looks after its construction and is your point of contact throughout. Kyle Leavitt walks every job site himself, and you follow the schedule, selections, inspections and change orders in Leavitt Connect, our homeowner app.

What is included in the price?

Every home includes the Leavitt Standard: 9-foot ceilings, quartz countertops, Andersen windows, hardwood or luxury vinyl plank flooring, a finished and painted garage and more, at no upgrade cost.

Can I change one of your plans?

Yes. Every plan is a starting point. The elevation, room sizes and optional spaces are adjusted to your lot and the way you want to live, and you choose the finishes, fixtures, cabinetry and flooring. See the plans.

Where do you build?

Harford County and the surrounding counties: Baltimore, Carroll, Cecil and Anne Arundel, and into southern Pennsylvania. We are based in Forest Hill, Maryland.
